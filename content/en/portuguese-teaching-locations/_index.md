Barbara Sharon teaches Portuguese in person only at suitable Gold Coast venues in Kirra, Broadbeach, and Surfers Paradise. These venues serve learners from across the Gold Coast; current venue and class availability are confirmed before booking.

Learners across the Gold Coast can attend in-person group classes in Kirra, Broadbeach, or Surfers Paradise, subject to current availability. Private in-person lessons are also arranged at a confirmed Gold Coast venue.

Brisbane and surrounding areas can still access in-person lessons in Surfers Paradise, although online lessons may be more suitable because of the distance. Online lessons are available for students everywhere else.
