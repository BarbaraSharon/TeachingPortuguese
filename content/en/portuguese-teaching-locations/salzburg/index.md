## Portuguese as a Global Language

Portuguese is one of the world’s most spoken languages, ranking fifth globally with over 267 million native speakers. It is not only the official language of Brazil, Portugal, Angola, Mozambique, and other countries but also serves as an important lingua franca in many parts of Africa, Asia, and the Americas.

Learning Portuguese in Salzburg gives you access to a dynamic linguistic tradition that reflects the vibrant cultures of these regions. Local interest in Portuguese is evident through the presence of language schools and cultural events like Festa do Brasil, which show growing demand for those seeking to connect with Lusophone communities. Whether your goal is travel, business, or personal enrichment, learning Portuguese opens doors to Brazil’s rich musical heritage, cinematic legacy, literary works, and social customs.
