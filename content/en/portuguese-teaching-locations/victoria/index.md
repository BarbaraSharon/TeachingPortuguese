## Why Learn Portuguese in Victoria?

Victoria's international character makes it an ideal place for language learning. With over 31% of residents born abroad, the city fosters a multicultural environment that supports language acquisition and cultural exchange.

The presence of organizations like the Brazilian Community Association of Alberta (BCAAB) highlights the active Brazilian community in Victoria. Events such as Carnaval, Festa Junina, and BrazilFest offer real-life opportunities to immerse yourself in the culture while practicing your Portuguese.

Additionally, with Canada-Brazil bilateral trade reaching $12.7 billion in 2024, learning Portuguese can open doors for personal or professional growth in this growing economic partnership.
