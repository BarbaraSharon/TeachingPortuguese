## Why Learn Portuguese in Tokyo?

Tokyo’s diverse international community creates unique opportunities for language learning. Although located in Japan, the city's global outlook allows learners to connect with Portuguese-speaking cultures from Brazil and Portugal. The Brazilian Chamber of Commerce in Japan (CCBJ) organizes events like the Brazil & Latin Festival at Yoyogi Park, celebrating 130 years of diplomatic relations between Brazil and Japan in 2025. These cultural gatherings provide immersive experiences for learners to engage with Brazilian music, cuisine, and traditions.

Brazil, home to the largest Portuguese-speaking population in the world, offers insights into the evolution and usage of the language. Learning Portuguese in Tokyo gives you access to the cultural nuances of Brazilian music, cinema, literature, and everyday communication styles that differ from European Portuguese. The Associação Portuguesa de Tóquio (APT) also hosts events like Festa Junina, offering a deeper understanding of Portuguese heritage through traditional dance, food, and art.

The city hosts several Brazilian community events including the Brazil & Latin Festival at Yoyogi Park and the Brazil Samba Dance Festival, both organized by the CCBJ. These celebrations offer a vibrant cultural backdrop for those interested in deepening their understanding of Portuguese-speaking communities.

## Portuguese as a Global Language

As the world's fifth-most spoken language, Portuguese is an important global lingua franca. With over 267 million native speakers, it's one of the most widely spoken Romance languages globally. Learning Portuguese in Tokyo gives you access to a rich linguistic tradition that extends beyond Brazil. The language has influenced and been influenced by various cultures across Africa, Asia, and the Americas, making it a fascinating subject for study.

Tokyo's growing trade links with Brazil and Portugal create additional motivation for learning Portuguese. Brazil and Japan maintain strong economic ties, with significant bilateral commerce in agriculture, automotive parts, and electronics. Meanwhile, Portugal and Japan have established strategic partnerships focusing on technology and green energy initiatives. These connections highlight the practical value of Portuguese language skills for professionals in Tokyo.
