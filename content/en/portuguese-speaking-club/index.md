## Portuguese Speaking Club - Build Fluency and Confidence

Ready to take your Portuguese to the next level? The Portuguese Speaking Club is an engaging, supportive group environment where you can practise real conversation, build confidence, and develop your fluency with fellow Portuguese learners.

The Portuguese Speaking Club helps beginner and advanced learners practise Brazilian Portuguese, improve pronunciation, and expand vocabulary. Sessions may be online or in person, depending on availability. Barbara Sharon is a qualified Brazilian Portuguese teacher and mental health counsellor who creates a supportive space for real conversation.

## How the Portuguese Speaking Club Works
- Conversation practice: Opportunities to use Portuguese in a friendly, supportive setting
- Small groups: Intimate groups give each participant time to contribute
- Themed topics: Conversations may explore travel, food, culture, business, current events, and more
- Task-based learning: Activities can include role-plays, debates, storytelling, and problem-solving challenges
- NLP and mind mapping: Teaching methods that support retention and fluency
- Integrated fun: Cooking, painting, music, and body language activities can make learning enjoyable

## Who Is the Speaking Club For?
- Beginner learners: Building confidence with spoken Portuguese and everyday conversation
- Advanced learners: Looking to refine accent, expand vocabulary, and sound more natural
- Travelers: Wanting to practice phrases they've learned in a live conversation setting
- Professionals: Needing to build business Portuguese skills through practical scenarios
- Culture enthusiasts: Passionate about Brazil and wanting to deepen cultural understanding through language

The Speaking Club welcomes learners at different stages who want to develop fluency through active use. You don't need to be a perfect speaker to join; you just need the willingness to practise and engage.

## What You'll Gain
- Increased fluency: Speaking practice builds confidence and reduces hesitation
- Better pronunciation: Feedback from a native Brazilian Portuguese teacher corrects accent issues
- Expanded vocabulary: Exposure to diverse topics introduces new words and expressions
- Cultural insight: Learn cultural nuances that make your Portuguese authentic
- Listening skills: Practice understanding different accents and speaking speeds
- Community: Connect with fellow Portuguese learners around the world
- Encouragement: A supportive learning environment helps you stay engaged with your goals
## Session Examples
### Recent Topics
- Planning a trip to Brazil - discussing itineraries, budgeting, and packing
- Brazilian cuisine - describing dishes, cooking techniques, and regional specialties
- Carnival and festivals - expressing opinions, telling stories, debating preferences
- Business Portuguese - negotiations, presentations, and professional networking
- Culture shock - sharing experiences, comparing countries, and reflecting on differences
- Movie and book reviews - giving opinions, describing plots, recommending titles
- Current events - discussing news, forming arguments, and practicing debate

Speaking activities are designed to stretch your confidence while keeping the atmosphere friendly and supportive. Barbara Sharon guides the conversation, corrects mistakes gently, and makes space for everyone to participate.

## Why Join the Speaking Club?

Many language learners reach a plateau where they understand Portuguese but struggle to speak fluently. The Speaking Club helps break through this barrier with a low-pressure environment to practise. Unlike private lessons, it exposes you to multiple speakers, different accents, and natural conversation flow - exactly what you need to sound authentic.

As a certified Portuguese teacher and mental health counsellor, Barbara Sharon creates a welcoming atmosphere where mistakes are celebrated as learning opportunities. Her unique approach combines task-based learning, NLP techniques, and mind mapping to make every session productive and enjoyable.

Say goodbye to language barriers and hello to greater fluency. Contact Barbara to learn about the current Speaking Club options and discover how much further your Portuguese can go.
