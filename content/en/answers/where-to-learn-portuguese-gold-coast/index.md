## Start with the lesson format

Gold Coast learners can consider three practical formats:

1. **Private in-person lessons** for individual goals, direct feedback and a pace set around one learner.
2. **Group classes** for shared practice, structured activities and regular conversation with other students.
3. **Live online lessons** when location, work or family commitments make travelling difficult.

In-person places and schedules can change, so confirm current availability before planning around a venue. Online lessons remain an option for adults who live elsewhere on the Gold Coast or need more scheduling flexibility.

## Compare a course before enrolling

Ask the teacher how lessons will address your starting level and main purpose. A useful course should explain:

- whether it teaches Brazilian or European Portuguese;
- how much live speaking and pronunciation practice is included;
- how grammar and vocabulary are connected to real situations;
- whether feedback and between-lesson practice are provided;
- which private, group, online or in-person formats are currently available.

If your goal is a trip, compare the course with the specific skills covered in [Portuguese for travel](/en/portuguese-for-travel/). If you mainly want speaking practice, review the [Portuguese Speaking Club](/en/portuguese-speaking-club/) as a separate conversation option.
