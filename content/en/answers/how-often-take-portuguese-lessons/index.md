## Start with the practice you can sustain

A lesson schedule only works when it leaves enough time to use what you learned. If several sessions introduce new language faster than you can review it, the extra frequency may not produce better retention. If sessions are so far apart that every lesson begins by rebuilding forgotten material, more regular contact may help.

Consider four questions:

1. **What is the goal?** A defined trip or event may require a different short-term plan from open-ended conversation development.
2. **How much independent practice is realistic?** Include work, family and other commitments.
3. **How much feedback do you need?** A beginner or learner changing pronunciation habits may need more regular correction.
4. **What can you attend consistently?** A modest routine maintained over time is preferable to repeated cancellations.

## Connect lessons with shorter practice

Think of a lesson as a feedback and planning point within a wider cycle:

- recall the previous lesson before the next session;
- practise speaking in brief, focused periods;
- listen to examples related to the current topic;
- note questions or repeated difficulties;
- reuse corrected sentences in a new situation.

Short sessions across the week help you meet the language repeatedly. The goal is not to count minutes for their own sake but to retrieve and use Portuguese before it becomes unfamiliar again.

## When a higher frequency may help

More frequent lessons may be useful when you have a genuine deadline, can practise between sessions and need feedback on a narrow goal. Examples include preparing for travel conversations, rehearsing a presentation or rebuilding confidence before meeting Portuguese-speaking family.

Higher frequency is less useful when it removes all independent practice time or cannot be maintained. Avoid treating intensity as a guarantee of fluency.

## When to adjust your schedule

Review the arrangement after several lessons. If you are completing practice comfortably and need more feedback, consider increasing contact. If you arrive without having reused the previous material, simplify the practice plan or allow more time. If conversation is the main need, a [Speaking Club](/en/portuguese-speaking-club/) may supplement rather than replace structured instruction.
