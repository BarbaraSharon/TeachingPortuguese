## “Best” depends on the learner

There is no honest universal ranking for a language tutor. A teacher who is a strong fit for a beginner preparing to visit Brazil may not be the right choice for an advanced learner refining professional writing. Treat “best” as a decision about fit, not a marketing label.

## Five criteria to compare

1. **Language variety:** Confirm whether the tutor teaches Brazilian or European Portuguese and whether that matches your goal.
2. **Relevant qualifications:** Look for a clear teaching and language background, not a vague claim of expertise.
3. **Active lesson time:** Ask how lessons balance guided conversation, pronunciation, listening, grammar and vocabulary.
4. **Useful feedback:** Find out how the teacher corrects errors and helps you apply feedback between lessons.
5. **Evidence and logistics:** Read attributable student feedback, then check format, scheduling and current in-person availability.

The [guide to learning Portuguese](/en/howto-learn-portuguese/) offers a broader framework for building a regular learning routine after you choose a teacher.

## Questions to ask before deciding

- What would the first few lessons focus on for my level and goal?
- How often will I speak Portuguese during a lesson?
- What practice should I do between sessions?
- Can the format change if my schedule or goal changes?
- Are Gold Coast in-person places currently available?
