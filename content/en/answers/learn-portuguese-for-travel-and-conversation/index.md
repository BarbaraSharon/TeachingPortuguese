## Build around real travel situations

Start with the conversations most likely to affect your trip. Greetings and courtesy matter, but so do checking into accommodation, understanding transport information, ordering food, asking for directions and explaining a problem. The [Portuguese for travel course](/en/portuguese-for-travel/) organises these practical areas with cultural guidance.

For an accessible starting list, review the [beginner Portuguese phrases](/en/portuguese-beginner-phrases/). Treat each phrase as a pattern you can change, not as a sentence you can use only once.

## Practise both sides of the conversation

It is not enough to prepare what you will say. You also need to recognise likely replies. For each scenario:

1. learn a short opening question or request;
2. listen to two or three realistic responses;
3. practise asking for repetition or clarification;
4. change one detail and repeat the exchange;
5. get feedback on pronunciation and word choice.

This approach makes your language more flexible when a real conversation does not follow a script.

## Choose the relevant Portuguese variety

Brazilian and European Portuguese are mutually understandable, but pronunciation, vocabulary and some everyday expressions differ. Choose the variety connected to your main destination while learning to recognise common alternatives. The [Brazilian and European Portuguese comparison](/en/brazilian-vs-european-portuguese/) explains the distinction in more detail.

## Add regular guided speaking

A teacher can select phrases for your itinerary, model natural pronunciation and role-play likely situations. Barbara’s travel lessons can be tailored to a learner’s destination and goals. [Online Portuguese lessons](/en/portuguese-teaching-services/online-portuguese-lessons/) allow adults worldwide to practise live before travelling.

Learners with an existing foundation can also consider the [Portuguese Speaking Club](/en/portuguese-speaking-club/) for continued conversation practice. It is a speaking option, not a substitute for every beginner foundation lesson.

## Prepare a compact travel routine

Use short daily sessions in the weeks before departure: listen, repeat, recall without notes and role-play. Keep a small personal list covering your accommodation, transport, dietary needs, destinations and emergency details. Then [contact Barbara](/en/contact-portuguese-teacher/) to discuss a lesson plan that reflects your trip and current level.
