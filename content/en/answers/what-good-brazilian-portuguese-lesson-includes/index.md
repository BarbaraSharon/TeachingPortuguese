## Begin with an outcome, not a list of topics

“Study the past tense” is a topic. “Tell a short story about last weekend and answer follow-up questions” is a communication outcome. A useful lesson connects the language feature to something the learner will understand or say.

## A practical lesson checklist

- **Purpose:** One clear, level-appropriate outcome.
- **Input:** A short conversation, text or model that the learner can understand with support.
- **Active use:** Time to retrieve and speak, not only watch or repeat explanations.
- **Pronunciation and listening:** Attention to sounds, rhythm and connected speech inside meaningful phrases.
- **Vocabulary and grammar:** Patterns selected because they support the current conversation.
- **Feedback:** A small number of specific corrections, followed by another chance to use them.
- **Follow-up:** A manageable task that revisits the lesson rather than introducing unrelated material.

Not every component needs equal time in every session. A beginner may need more modelling; an advanced learner may spend longer refining nuance or responding spontaneously. The important point is that activities serve the goal.

## What to notice as a student

By the end, you should be able to identify something you can now understand, say or improve. You should also know which correction matters most and what to practise next. Entertainment and variety can support attention, but a busy lesson is not automatically a coherent one.

## Barbara’s teaching perspective

Barbara describes her lessons as a combination of conversation, pronunciation, vocabulary, grammar and cultural context adapted to the learner’s background and objective. Her approach uses feedback and review to turn explanation into language the student can use.

Compare this checklist with the [online lesson guide](/en/answers/best-online-brazilian-portuguese-lessons/) or [discuss your goal with Barbara](/en/contact-portuguese-teacher/). Ask what you would practise first, how feedback works and what is expected between sessions.
