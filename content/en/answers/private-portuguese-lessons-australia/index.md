## Online lessons make private tuition accessible across Australia

A live online lesson removes the need to find a teacher in the same suburb or city. It can still be a private, interactive session with real-time conversation, correction and a plan built around one learner. Check that “online course” means live teaching if personal feedback is important to you; a recorded course offers a different experience.

Barbara’s [online Portuguese lessons](/en/portuguese-teaching-services/online-portuguese-lessons/) are available to adults worldwide, including learners across Australian time zones. Scheduling depends on current availability.

## Gold Coast learners can ask about in-person tutoring

Barbara also offers [private Portuguese tutoring on the Gold Coast](/en/portuguese-teaching-services/portuguese-tutoring-gold-coast/) subject to availability. The [Gold Coast location guide](/en/portuguese-teaching-locations/gold-coast/) explains the local service area. Other Australian location pages describe online access and should not be read as a promise of an in-person venue in each city.

## What to compare in a private lesson

Before choosing a teacher, ask about:

- the variety of Portuguese being taught;
- qualifications and relevant teaching experience;
- how the lesson will reflect your level and purpose;
- the balance of conversation, pronunciation, listening, grammar and vocabulary;
- how feedback and independent practice are handled;
- current scheduling and delivery format.

Private lessons can be especially useful when your goal is specific, such as speaking with family, preparing for travel, improving pronunciation or using Portuguese at work. The broader [Portuguese teaching services overview](/en/portuguese-teaching-services/) helps compare private and group formats.

## How Barbara’s private lessons work

Barbara teaches Brazilian Portuguese and adapts lesson content to the learner’s level and goal. Her verified background is described on the [about Barbara page](/en/about-learning-portuguese/). She offers online lessons for adults worldwide and in-person Gold Coast options subject to availability.

When you [contact Barbara](/en/contact-portuguese-teacher/), include your city or time zone, current level, main goal and preferred lesson times. That information makes it easier to confirm whether a suitable private format is currently available.
