## Why one advertised price does not tell the whole story

Two lessons with the same duration may provide very different experiences. One may be an individual live session with corrections and a plan built around your goal. Another may be a larger group, a recorded course or a conversation session with less individual feedback. Compare like with like before deciding that one option is cheaper.

The main cost factors usually include:

- **Format:** private teaching normally allocates all lesson time to one learner, while group costs are shared.
- **Delivery:** online teaching removes travel, but it should still provide live interaction if personal correction is important to you.
- **Lesson length and frequency:** a sustainable schedule can be more valuable than booking more sessions than you can prepare for.
- **Teacher background:** qualifications, experience and the ability to adapt explanations contribute to the service.
- **What is included:** ask about materials, feedback, practice between lessons and communication outside the scheduled session.

## Questions to ask when comparing a quote

Request the current price for the exact format you need and ask:

1. Is the lesson private, shared or part of a larger class?
2. How long is each live session?
3. Are learning materials or follow-up activities included?
4. How are cancellations and rescheduling handled?
5. Will the lesson be adapted to my level and goal?
6. What practice is expected between sessions?

This gives you a practical basis for comparing teachers without relying on an unverified price found on an old page or directory listing.

## Choose value according to your goal

A traveller preparing for a specific trip may value targeted role-play and pronunciation feedback. A beginner may need a clear sequence of lessons and regular correction. Someone maintaining conversational Portuguese may prefer a group environment with varied speakers. The [private-versus-group comparison](/en/answers/private-vs-group-portuguese-lessons/) can help you choose the format before requesting prices.
