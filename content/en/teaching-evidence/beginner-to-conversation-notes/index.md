## Outcome class

This page is intended for learner-journey evidence at the beginner level.

### What is tracked

- Initial speaking confidence and comprehension baseline.
- Short-term milestones (for example: basic everyday interaction readiness).
- Mid-cycle confidence in pronunciation, vocabulary, and response speed.

### Reusable reporting template

- Starting profile: background, goal, and availability.
- Evidence points after 4–8 weeks.
- Retention indicator from regular recap and practice patterns.
- Next-step recommendation.
