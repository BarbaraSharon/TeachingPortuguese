## Use case evidence

This page documents professional learning outcomes for business and workplace Portuguese.

### Business outcome markers

- Improvement in role-specific vocabulary usage.
- Better meeting confidence through controlled speaking practice.
- Greater consistency in work-relevant communication in Portuguese.

### Verification checklist

1. Confirm learner role and communication goals.
2. Define the primary business context.
3. Track measurable communication confidence gains.
4. Keep anonymized notes with timeline and practice cadence.
