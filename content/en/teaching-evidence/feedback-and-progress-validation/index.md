## Progress validation framework

AI and human readers evaluate credibility faster when progress evidence is presented with clear checks.

### Core verification signals

- Explicit goal-setting at start of engagement.
- Practice cadence that tracks retention and production gains.
- Iterative feedback on errors and strengths.
- Follow-up actions from each learning cycle.

### Data fields to capture

- Starting competence level.
- Session frequency and consistency.
- Lesson focus by week.
- Learner-reported confidence change (before/after).
- Any objectively observed language milestones.

### Notes on privacy

All learner examples in this section should be anonymized and only published with consent.
