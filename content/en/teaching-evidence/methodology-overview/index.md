## Method evidence

This page documents the teaching methodology framework currently used for core Portuguese lessons.

### Core method principles

- Language exposure in speaking, listening, reading, and writing each week.
- Early correction with practical follow-up and immediate recap.
- Explicit progress checkpoints so learners can see what has improved.
- Task-based practice aligned to learner goals (travel, business, study, everyday communication).
