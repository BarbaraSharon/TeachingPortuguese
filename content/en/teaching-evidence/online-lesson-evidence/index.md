## Scope of evidence

This page records delivery practices for online lessons and how progress is maintained between sessions.

### Delivery facts

- Online lessons are offered internationally for learners outside Australia.
- Session structure is typically goal-driven with recurring review cycles.
- Learners are encouraged to practice regularly to reinforce retention.

### Supporting observations

- Course pages consistently identify online formats, scheduling flexibility, and progression goals.
- FAQ responses repeatedly emphasize regular speaking practice and consistent study cadence.

### Evidence format

For each anonymized learner journey, include:

1. Starting level and goal.
2. Session frequency and weekly review pattern.
3. Verifiable outcomes (for example: improved speaking confidence or specific competency gains).
4. Retention signals from recurring feedback.
