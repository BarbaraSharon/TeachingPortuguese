### Essential Greetings & Polite Expressions

Start every interaction positively with proper greetings and manners:
- Bom dia (Good morning), Boa tarde (Good afternoon), Boa noite (Good evening)
- Olá, Oi (Hello/Hi)
- Por favor (Please), Obrigada/Obrigado (Thank you)
- Desculpe/Sinto muito (Excuse me/I'm sorry)
- Sim/Não (Yes/No)
- Prazer em conhecê-lo (Nice to meet you)
### Travel & Transportation

Navigate with confidence from airport to accommodation:
- Onde fica...? (Where is...?)
- Quanto custa para ir ao aeroporto? (How much to get to the airport?)
- Passagem de avião/trem/ônibus (Plane/train/bus ticket)
- Hotel/pousada/albergue (Hotel/guest house/hostel)
- Táxi, Uber, aplicativo de transporte (Taxi, Uber, ride-sharing app)
- Perto/Afaste (Near/Far)
### Dining & Food

Order like a local and discover authentic Brazilian and Portuguese cuisine:
- A carta/cardápio, por favor (The menu, please)
- Uma mesa para duas pessoas (A table for two people)
- O que você recomenda? (What do you recommend?)
- Sem carne de porco / Sem laticínios / Vegetariano (No pork / No dairy / Vegetarian)
- A conta, por favor (The bill, please)
- Gostei muito da comida (I really liked the food)
### Shopping & Bargaining

Navigate markets and shops with confidence:
- Quanto custa? / Quanto é? (How much does it cost?)
- Muito caro! (Too expensive!)
- Pode fazer um preço melhor? (Can you make a better price?)
- Eu só tenho... (I only have...)
- Aceita cartão? (Do you accept cards?)
### Emergency & Safety Phrases

Know what to say in urgent situations:
- Preciso de ajuda! (I need help!)
- Ajuda médica! (Medical help!)
- Chame uma ambulância/ polícia (Call an ambulance/police)
- Perdi meu passaporte/documentos (I lost my passport/documents)
- Onde fica a embaixada? (Where is the embassy?)
### Cultural & Social Interaction

Connect with locals and understand cultural nuances:
- Você fala inglês? (Do you speak English?)
- Não entendo, pode repetir mais devagar? (I don't understand, can you repeat more slowly?)
- Como se diz... em português? (How do you say... in Portuguese?)
- Sua língua é linda! (Your language is beautiful!)
### Brazilian vs European Portuguese Variants

I tailor the course to your destination, teaching:
- Brazilian Portuguese: For Brazil travel (clearer pronunciation, more commonly used globally)
- European Portuguese: For Portugal and its islands (local expressions and pronunciation)
- Differences in vocabulary for common items between variants

## Frequently Asked Questions
### How long before my trip should I start learning?

I recommend starting at least 4-6 weeks before departure for a focused travel course. However, even shorter programs can help you learn essential survival phrases that will make your trip more enjoyable.
### I've never studied a language before-can I still take this course?

Absolutely! My teaching approach is designed for all experience levels. The travel course focuses on practical phrases rather than complex grammar, making it accessible even to complete beginners.
### Will I be able to understand locals if they speak quickly?

The course includes listening comprehension practice with various speaking speeds. While perfect fluency isn't realistic for short courses, you'll develop strategies for handling fast speech and knowing when to ask for clarification.
### What's the difference between Brazilian and European Portuguese for travelers?

Brazilian Portuguese is more widely spoken (206 million vs 10 million speakers) and generally easier for beginners due to clearer pronunciation. However, learning the variant specific to your destination shows cultural respect. I'll teach you the appropriate variant based on where you're traveling.
### Can you customize lessons for my specific trip?

Yes! Before starting, we'll discuss your itinerary, planned activities, and any concerns you have about communication barriers. I'll then tailor the curriculum to focus on those specific situations-whether that's hiking tours in Rio, wine tastings in Portugal, or beach vacations in Bahia.
### Do you offer group lessons for families or travel groups?

I do! Group lessons can be a fun and cost-effective option for families traveling together. Please contact me to discuss your group's needs and scheduling options.
